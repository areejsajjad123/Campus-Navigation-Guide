What We Offer

Everything You Need
to Navigate Campus

A complete web-based system designed for students, visitors, and staff of Govt. Islamia Graduate College.

Interactive Campus Map

View the full campus map with markers for every building, department, lab, and office. Powered by Leaflet.js and OpenStreetMap.

Smart Location Search

Type any location name and instantly get results pinned on the map with building details, timings, and contact info.

Route Guidance

Get the shortest route from your current GPS location to any destination on campus in seconds.

Live Location Detection

Allow browser GPS access to see exactly where you are on campus and navigate from your current position.

Online Help Desk

Submit queries and complaints directly through the system. No need to visit offices physically.

Admin Dashboard

Administrators can manage campus locations, view all user queries, and respond with full control.
